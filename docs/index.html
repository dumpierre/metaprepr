How it works

1

Enter what you have

Whatever your source reports: median and range, Q1/Q3, SE, or a 95% CI. Irrelevant fields hide themselves.

2

Pick a method

The app tells you which scenario it detected (S1/S2/S3) and defaults to the method the literature recommends.

3

Export your workspace

Every result lands in one editable table. Download as CSV or XLSX when your dataset is ready.
